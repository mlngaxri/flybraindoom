SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARY

What is real, and what is still an adapter

Closed loop now

Each fly has its own connectome worker, its own neural state, its own learned readout and its own egocentric opponent stimulus. The policy never receives opponent coordinates, distance, score or arena state directly. Those hidden values are used only to create sensory stimuli, resolve contact and calculate reinforcement.

High-fidelity target

The repository also pins a MaleCNS WebGPU adapter contract and a FlyGym 2.1.0 MuJoCo-WASM adapter contract. They are not labelled active until the actual MaleCNS browser weights and a two-fly NeuroMechFly shared-world model are loaded and validated.

Competition

“Fighting” means approach, retreat, turning, lunging, contact and knockback. Stability is a game variable, not biological injury. Contact generates a modeled aversive sensory pulse; this does not imply subjective pain or consciousness.
