METHOD

What is real vs modelled

Measured / sourced

Connectome topology, neuron positions, neuron metadata and LC4/LPLC2 receptive-field assignments come from the browser-ready FlyWire-derived dataset used by Fruit Fly Lab.

Freedoom runs through a WebAssembly Chocolate Doom build.

Engineered

Doom frames are temporally adapted, separated into rotation and forward optic-flow components, then converted into spatial looming candidates that drive the simulator's LC4/LPLC2 encoder.

A small reinforcement readout learns from neural telemetry only. Learning state is saved locally across reloads. Weapon firing is disabled.
