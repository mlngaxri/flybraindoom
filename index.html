METHOD

What “scary for the fly” means

Measured / sourced

Connectome topology, neuron positions, neuron metadata and LC4/LPLC2 receptive-field assignments come from the browser-ready FlyWire-derived dataset used by Fruit Fly Lab.

Freedoom runs through a WebAssembly Chocolate Doom build. The controller remains navigation-only, with no weapon-firing action.

Engineered threat model

The software does not assume the simulated fly subjectively feels fear. Instead, rapid image expansion, abrupt darkening and strong local motion are amplified into looming stimuli intended to recruit escape/freeze-related activity more strongly.

The reinforcement readout still receives neural telemetry only. Full-frame game pixels are used for sensory encoding and environmental reward signals, not as direct policy inputs.
