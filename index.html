METHOD

What the fly actually controls

Environment

Blocks are generated procedurally with random hand, position, speed, spacing and rotation. Blue targets occupy a random location in the left visual field and red targets a random location in the right, because the current connectome build models looming more faithfully than spectral colour. There is no song chart and no audio timing.

The smiling cube and both sabers are original procedural graphics. This project does not include Beat Saber code, music or art assets.

Neural loop

The full rendered frame is converted into spatial motion and looming stimuli for the fly-connectome simulator. The learned readout receives neural telemetry only.

The three available outputs are idle, swing the left blue saber, or swing the right red saber. Hidden block position, colour and timing are never passed directly to the policy.
