METHOD

What is real vs modelled

Measured / sourced

The connectome topology, neuron positions, neuron metadata and LC4/LPLC2 receptive-field assignments come from the browser-ready FlyWire-derived dataset used by Fruit Fly Lab.

The controller receives simulated neural telemetry from that connectome. The reinforcement policy never receives pipe coordinates, bird height, velocity or collision geometry.

Engineered

The Flappy-style environment, replacement artwork, neuronal dynamics, frame-to-visual-drive encoder and flap/coast readout are engineered components. No original Flappy Bird sprites, audio or source code are redistributed.

Reinforcement is event-based: small survival reward, large pipe-pass reward, bonus for a new best score, and a large collision penalty. This removes the old motion/rotation reward loopholes.
